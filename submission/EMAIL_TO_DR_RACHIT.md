**Proposed project:** EventFlow Twin – Agentic AI for Event-Ready Mobility in Dubai.
It is an interactive digital twin demonstrator: visitors use a touch screen to see metro-station crowding build up around a large event on the Sheikh Zayed Road / Trade Centre corridor, ask AI agents for a plan (extra trains, feeder shuttles, robotaxi staging, staggered exits), and approve it themselves.

**Built around three RTA AI Strategy priorities:**
- **Congestion reduction:** measures how many overcapacity station-hours each plan removes
- **Agentic AI:** agents plan end to end, while a simulator produces every number
- **Human approval:** nothing is applied until a planner approves it, and every decision is logged

It uses RTA's public open data (Dubai Pulse), with clearly labelled synthetic scenarios and no personal data. The exhibit needs one table and two sockets, and works fully offline.

Please find attached the updated nomination for EventFlow Twin (v4, 18 November 2026). It replaces the earlier
version and describes the prototype exactly as it runs today.

What has changed since the last version:
- The single planner is now a team of 18 AI agents (8 can call a language model, 10 are code only). A critic checks
  every plan on seven named criteria, and a deterministic simulator still produces every figure.
- The human is asked, not only informed. The team stops at decision points and asks the planner. Even when no station
  is over the comfort line, it tests harder cases itself and asks which one to plan for. Nothing is applied without an
  approval, and every approval is logged.
- Visitors can type on the kiosk with a built-in English and Arabic keyboard, change the scenario in words, describe
  their own event, and edit the AI's plan before approving it.
- Two of the four pictures are new: the kiosk keyboard, and the team's question to the visitor.
- All figures were measured again today. The headline scenario is unchanged: GITEX 2025 Day 1 peaks at 78% at World
  Trade Centre and tips over at about 44,400 visitors; on a hot day it reaches 92% and the recommended plan brings it
  to 74%, passing all 6 applicable checks. Every number in 132 of 132 plan options traces to the simulator, and 0 of
  53 attack cases succeeded.

What the nomination states plainly, so nothing is over-claimed:
- Station demand is synthetic and labelled; the timetable is RTA's official open data.
- No language model runs at the stand. The demo is fully offline, on rules and code.
- Typed questions are understood by word rules; in our test 17 of 24 unseen phrasings were routed correctly.
- It is a research prototype, not an RTA system, and not endorsed by RTA.

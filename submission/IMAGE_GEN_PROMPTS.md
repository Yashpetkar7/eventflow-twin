# Image-generation prompts (ChatGPT / any text-to-image tool)

## Rules before you generate anything
1. **Use AI images for mood only:** poster background, booth visual, social post. **Do not** use them as "evidence" in the RTA template. The template asks for original project photographs or clean renders, so use real screenshots from the prototype there (or the concept renders in `images/`, which are labelled).
2. **Label every AI image** "AI-generated concept image" in small text wherever it appears.
3. **No logos** (RTA, Dubai Metro, Dubai Future Week, Museum of the Future), no real people's faces, and no copyrighted characters.
4. **Add text yourself** afterwards in Canva/Figma/PowerPoint. Image generators misspell words, especially Arabic.
5. **Brand look:** light pearl background (#FAF8F5), deep transit blue (#1D4ED8), sand (#C9A46A), soft shadows. Calm, premium, analytical, not neon sci-fi.

## Fix-up prompts (if the first result is off)
- "Make it lighter and calmer: reduce glow by 50%, more white space, softer shadows."
- "Remove all text, signs and logos from the image."
- "Keep the composition but change the accent colour to deep blue #1D4ED8 only."
- "Make the buildings generic; nothing should look like a real landmark."

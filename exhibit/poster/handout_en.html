EventFlow Twin

Predict the crowd before it arrives – agentic AI for event-ready mobility in Dubai

What it is

An interactive digital twin of the Sheikh Zayed Road / Trade Centre corridor. Pick a big event such as GITEX, watch metro-station crowding build up hour by hour, ask AI agents for a plan, and decide yourself whether to approve it.

How it works

  1. Forecast station entries in 15-minute steps, plus the event surge
  2. Flag stations above 85 % of comfortable capacity
  3. A planning agent proposes options from a fixed menu
  4. A simulator measures each option – the AI never calculates
  5. A human approves; the twin shows before / after and logs it

Honest about the data

Train frequencies come from RTA's official open timetable (GTFS) and the city from OpenStreetMap. Station demand is synthetic and labelled until RTA's metro ridership – now open on data.dubai – is ingested. Attendance, metro share and train fullness are assumptions you can change on screen. This is a student research prototype designed for RTA's priorities – not an RTA system.

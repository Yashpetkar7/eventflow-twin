The full prototype uses RTA open data (Dubai Pulse). Not an RTA system and not endorsed by RTA. Arabic text is a draft for native-speaker review.

    How EventFlow Twin works

    1. Forecast: background demand per station and hour, plus an event surge shared across stations by walking distance.
    2. Capacity: trains per hour × train capacity × the share available at one station. Station-hours above the comfort threshold are flagged.
    3. AI agents propose plans from a fixed menu: extra trains, wayfinding, feeder shuttles, robotaxi staging and staggered exits.
    4. A deterministic simulator scores every plan. The agents never calculate numbers; every figure is checked.
    5. Nothing is applied until a human approves it, and every decision is logged.

    Built around RTA AI Strategy priorities: congestion reduction, agentic AI and human approval of every decision.
